# Billing and invoices

## Invoice schedule

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month. Payment is due within thirty days of
the invoice date. Customers on annual contracts receive one invoice per year.

## Late payments

A late fee of 1.5 percent per month applies to overdue balances. Accounts more than sixty days
overdue are suspended until the balance is paid.

## Disputes

Customers can dispute an invoice line within fourteen days by emailing the billing team. Disputed
amounts are not subject to late fees while the dispute is open.
