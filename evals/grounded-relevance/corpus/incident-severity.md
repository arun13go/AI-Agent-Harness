# Incident severity levels

| Severity | Meaning | First response | Update cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sev1 | Complete outage or data loss | 15 minutes | Every 30 minutes |
| Sev2 | Major feature unavailable | 30 minutes | Every hour |
| Sev3 | Minor degradation with a workaround | 4 hours | Daily |
| Sev4 | Cosmetic issue | Next business day | None |

## Declaring an incident

Any engineer can declare an incident. The incident commander assigns the severity, and the severity
can be raised or lowered as facts change.

## Postmortems

Sev1 and Sev2 incidents require a written postmortem within five business days.
